# Heating season
```text
Central heating in Lithuania is turned on when average temperatures drop below 10 degrees three days in a row.
Heating is turned off when average temperatures gets above 10 degrees three days in a row.
```

# For students who apply for TRP

* To get TRP as a student you need to provide a proof finace - bank account with 6x minimum monthly salary. (preffered in local bank)
* **Renewing TRP** must be done before the current TRP expires.
* If your TRP expired but you applied for a new TRP withing 6 month from its expiration, you will not have to provide a criminal history record again.
